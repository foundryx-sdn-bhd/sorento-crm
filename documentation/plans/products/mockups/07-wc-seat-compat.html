Where it lives

  • Products > All Products > open any product > Variants tab. The section sits directly under Alternatives, on every product. There is no separate page and no new sidebar item.
  • A WC shows "Seat covers that fit"; a seat cover shows "Fits these WCs". The badge says whether a link came from the flyer import (Flyer) or a person (Manual).
  • Add compatible: pick the other product and whether it is a seat cover or a WC, one SKU at a time. Unlink is the usual 5s countdown with Cancel. It needs the existing products edit permission.

The flyer itself

  • No review page. A one-time script, sorento_crm_backend/scripts/oneoff/link_seat_cover_flyer.py, carries the 49 printed flyer pairs, links only the SKUs a printed row names unambiguously, and prints the unresolved ones with their reason. Dry run by default; --apply writes the links; --retire-spec then removes the old Seat Cover spec values.

Existing components only

  • Product page and tabs: master-data-management/products/[id] (unchanged). Section: products/[id]/components/ProductCompatibilitySection.tsx, the same row and empty-state markup as ProductAlternativesSection.tsx, placed in ProductVariantsTab.tsx. Add: ProductVariantPickerDialog.tsx. Badges: components/ui/badge.tsx. Unlink countdown: hooks/useDeferredRowAction.tsx.
