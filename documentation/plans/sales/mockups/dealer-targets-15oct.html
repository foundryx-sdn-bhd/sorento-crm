Dealer targets and incentive schemes

Lane SALES-TARGET-DEALER, slices 1 to 3. Dealers get a third tab in Sales > Targets, a dealer target lists the dealer's accounts, its tiers are fixed RM thresholds per quarter (or year, or month) with a rate per account, an optional "+ Additional x%" line, and Mocha schemes pay in points. The Excel import keys on customer code and always shows a Test preview first. Figures and dealer names are placeholders until the masked Excel rows arrive. Pins map to the notes; each note names the component reused and its repo path.
