3. What CS does next

  1. Re-decide on the board: another source (own location, borrow, site pool), Confirm. Purchasing is not notified (they have nothing to buy).
  2. Buy again: keep Buy, type why in REASON, Save, Confirm. Purchasing gets the usual new order inquiry; its "Why this instruction" reads CS: <your reason> (note 2).
  3. Cancel the line: ✕ or Cancel SO with its reason, Confirm.
  4. Change the item in AutoCount when the customer takes a substitute, re-sync; the board shows "Change proposed"; Confirm sends the new item to purchasing as a normal order inquiry.
