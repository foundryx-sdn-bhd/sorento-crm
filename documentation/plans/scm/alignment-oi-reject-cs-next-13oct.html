2. What CS can do next, with what exists today

  1. Re-decide on the board: open the line, choose another source (own location, borrow, site pool) and Confirm. The refusal clears once the line is decided again. board, today
  2. Ask purchasing again: keep Buy and Confirm. A fresh order inquiry row goes to purchasing. CS's Reason is kept on the board's decision (Decision trail) but does not reach purchasing's order inquiry row today. board, today
  3. Cancel the line: Decide, Cancel SO, with the mandatory reason, Confirm. The line is retired. board, today
  4. Change the item in AutoCount (e.g. the customer accepts another finish): edit the SO line in AutoCount, re-sync the SO book. The board shows the line as "Change proposed"; CS confirms it and the new item goes to purchasing. AutoCount, then board
  5. Cancel the line in AutoCount: re-sync; the board shows it Cancelled; Confirm retires it. AutoCount, then board

Recommended default: CS calls the customer, then (4) when the customer takes a substitute item, otherwise (1) or (3) on the board. (2) only when purchasing's reason is temporary (factory closed, back-order).

3. Questions for the owner (recommendations in bold)

  1. When CS buys a refused line again, should CS's Reason travel to purchasing's new order inquiry row (shown beside it on the Order Inquiries page)? Yes: otherwise purchasing sees the same request it just refused with no word on what changed. Small follow-up lane; no new button.
  2. Should an unanswered refusal remind the raiser after a few days? Not now: the bell, the email and the header count are new; measure first.
  3. Should purchasing be told when CS answers the refusal (re-decides, cancels or changes the item)? Yes, as a small follow-up lane: one bell to the rejecter, no email.
  4. Should the email list these next actions? No: the email links straight to the line; the how-to belongs in the Outline guide.
