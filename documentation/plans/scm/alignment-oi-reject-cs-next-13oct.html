After purchasing rejects: what CS sees and does next (v3)

PR #1622. v3 applies the owner's hand-test notes of 13 Oct: a rejection is an order inquiry status. It shows on the OI column as a red "Rejected" badge with an info icon, on the Sales Order's Lines tab and on the planning board. Verdict and Decided stay normal planning values. The email and bell open the Sales Order page.

1. Email and bell land on the Sales Order, Lines tab (point 2)

Email: the same single button, Open sales order, now linking to /scm/sales-orders/<SO id>?tab=lines&line=<first rejected line>. The bell notice opens the same place.

  • Decided No reminders; no extra notice to purchasing.
  • Built Footer reads Sorento.
