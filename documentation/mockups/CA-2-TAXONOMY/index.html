Questions (recommendation first)

  1. Who judges the sample? (a) a person marks all 300 (one click each, about 15 minutes); (b) a larger model relabels all 300 and a person reviews only the disagreements. Recommend (a): no extra model, and the precision numbers are a person's judgement.
  2. Who sees the customer's words on the audit? (a) anyone who already holds system.chat_history.view and chatbot.analytics.view; (b) a new grant. Recommend (a): the same people already read those words on the trace screen.
  3. How the 300 are drawn: (a) at least 10 per subject that has asks, the rest in proportion to volume, and half of that rest from the lowest-confidence labels; (b) purely random. Recommend (a): small subjects and doubtful labels are where errors hide.
  4. Bot failures report (failure reason by topic, plus the turn list): (a) include it in this lane, about one definition file and a menu line; (b) leave it to a later lane. Recommend (a): it is small and reuses the same datasets.
  5. Top asks opens on: (a) 5 Sep 2026 to today, topic by month, like Chat overview; (b) the last 30 days. Recommend (a): the month columns are the point of the view.
