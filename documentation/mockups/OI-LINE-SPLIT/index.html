2Split view (live)

Rows are the SO's lines, grouped by item and location; columns are delivery dates, then "New date" and "Cancel". Each card is one split. Drag a card to another date, to another line of the same item, to "New line" (AutoCount does not have it yet) or to "Cancel"; a small box asks how many (Enter = all). Each card keeps its own line: a card sitting on another line says "from the 417 line", and the row header says what moved where. Click a line to zoom it on the right: drag the dark dividers on its bar, type, right-click. Cmd/Ctrl+Z undoes. Save needs every line to add up exactly.
