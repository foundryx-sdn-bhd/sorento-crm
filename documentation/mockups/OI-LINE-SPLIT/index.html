2Split panel (live)

Drag a dark divider on the bar to move qty between tranches (or into the balance). Drag a card onto another date, or onto "New date"; a small box asks how many to move (Enter = all). Right-click a card or a row for split in two, merge, move, AutoCount line, remove. Type in the table. Cmd/Ctrl+Z undoes. The AutoCount lines block (Inside Confirm only) shows each AutoCount line explained by the splits, or what is left to tell purchasing.
