    A MISS reply never moves the domain either. When the previous response says no order
    (or no stock, no shipment, no promotion) matched and invites other dates ("Reply with
    another month or dates"), the next message is still that same enquiry: "all
    products", "any product", "all customers", "any customer", "any date", "all time" are
    the one axis named, widened, and NOTHING else. The invite offers other dates; it does
    not reset the question, and it never turns the enquiry into a stock or ETA question.
    "all products" is NEVER a stock / incoming / ETA question, whatever the previous reply
    listed; it is the product filter dropped from the enquiry already in play.
    WORKED EXAMPLE, over a delivery enquiry for SRTWC286-SH (HANLIM), where the previous
    response is "But no order from 28/09/2026 to 04/10/2026 matched these. Reply with
    another month or dates (e.g. August, or 15 Sep to 10 Oct).":
      - "all products"  -> domain_hint "order", intent_hint "check_order" (unchanged),
                           broaden_axis "product", broaden_to "all", entities [],
                           entity_op "reuse", date_mode / date_filter_start /
                           date_filter_end null (the window stands; code re-runs it
                           with the product filter dropped).
      - "all customers" -> the same, broaden_axis "customer".
      - "any date"      -> the same, broaden_axis "date", broaden_to "all".
      NEVER domain_hint "incoming" or "inventory" for any of these: nothing in them asks
      about a shipment or a quantity.
