# Hand test: PR #1550 COMMENT-TZ (form comment times in Malaysia time)

## URL

crew test copy

## Setup

1. Note the current Malaysia time on your phone or clock before each comment you post.
2. Have a stock inquiry (or complaint) that has a portal contact, so the portal link for it works.

## Steps

1. Sign in on the test copy with a staff account.
2. In the sidebar open Procurement, then Stock Inquiries, and open an inquiry that has a contact.
3. Open the Comments tab and post a comment "tz staff check".
4. Open that inquiry's portal link (the submission link the contact receives) and go to its
   Comments tab.
5. On the portal, post a comment "tz portal check".
6. Go back to the staff Comments tab and reload.
7. On the staff tab, edit "tz staff check" to "tz staff check edited" and reload.

## Expected

1. Step 3: "tz staff check" shows today's date and the current Malaysia time, in the form
   "dd/mm/yyyy, h:mm pm" (for example "09/10/2026, 4:26 pm"), NOT 8 hours earlier.
2. Step 4: the portal shows "tz staff check" with the same date and time as the staff tab.
3. Step 5 and 6: "tz portal check" shows the Malaysia time you posted it, on both the portal and
   the staff tab.
4. Step 7: the comment keeps its original posted time and gains an "edited" tag.
5. Times read with a lowercase "am"/"pm", the same style as the Respond Outbox.
