# Hand test: PR #1580 OI-CLOSED-LINE (the delivered part is never capped; a shortfall warns)

## URL

crew test copy

## Setup

Crew setup on the test copy of head 744b0fb4 (or later). The owner runs no commands.

1. No migration. The copy uses the shared dev DB.
2. Purchasing email safety: steps 11 and 12 queue the "Order inquiry to purchasing" handover
   email. Its recipients are the users holding a `purchasing*` role plus any `user_ids` or
   `extra_emails` an admin set on that automation row; on the dev DB (a production copy) those
   can be real mailboxes. The email is only queued (System, Outgoing Mails, status pending);
   only a process running with `ENABLE_SCHEDULER=true` drains it to SMTP
   (`app/tasks/email_outbox_tasks.py` `drain_email_outbox`). The copy's backend and worker run
   with `ENABLE_SCHEDULER` unset, so nothing is sent. Crew checks this before the owner starts.

## Steps

1. Open the URL and sign in as a CS planner.
2. In the sidebar, open Fulfilment Planning and find SO416732.
3. Tick the CB2807-DIY line.
4. Press Decide, then choose "Use BRW".
5. Type the reason "Delivered using BRW stock".
6. Press the Save button.
7. Open SO424764 and click line 144 (CGB9032B) to open the line editor.
8. Turn off "Buy the whole line" if it is on, then press Add location and add BRW-BB (or use its
   Reserve row if it already has one).
9. Type 98 into "Reserve at BRW-BB".
10. Turn on the "Order back" switch, type the reason "98 used from BB, 2 order back", and press
    Save decision.
11. Press Confirm for SO424764.
12. On any open line with nothing delivered, set Reserve above what its location shows free,
    save it with a reason, and press Confirm.
13. In the sidebar, open System, then Outgoing Mails, and open the newest pending mail.

## Expected

1. Step 4: the dialog reads "Decide 1 lines: Use BRW", with no "will be skipped" text. The
   button reads "Save 1 line" and is enabled once the reason is typed.
2. Step 6: a toast reads "1 saved as Use BRW".
3. Step 9: no "Only 77 available here" text; "Buy 2" shows under Buy.
4. Step 10: the Order back switch is visible next to the Reserve, and the line reads Saved.
5. Step 11: Confirm succeeds, with no "cannot be confirmed" refusal for line 144.
6. Step 12: Confirm succeeds. An amber toast says the line is short of stock ("1 short of
   stock" on the board, or "only N free at <location> when confirmed, M short").
7. Step 13: the mail's status is "pending" (not sent). Its red headline includes "STOCK SHORT",
   and its table has a row for the step 12 item whose REMARK reads "Reserve <qty> at <location>
   confirmed as decided; only N free at <location> when confirmed, M short".
