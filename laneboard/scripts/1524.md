5. Choose Transfer to Cancel SO.
6. Leave the reason empty and look at the Save button.

8. Expand line 384.
9. Press the Undo icon on line 416, then tick 416 again, Decide, Transfer to Cancel SO, reason CANCEL ORDER, Save.
