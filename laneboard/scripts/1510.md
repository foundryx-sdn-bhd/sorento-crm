## Setup

1. Restart the MCP server on the test copy (the availability wording comes from the MCP presenter; it is unchanged, the figure behind it is).
2. Chatbot Console contact DEALER: an "Availability only" stock policy contact scoped to ONE warehouse (hand tests #1430, #1461 and #1501 used CONTACT A, Respond.io 900000039; any availability contact whose policy names a warehouse works). Note its warehouse as `<WH>`.
3. Inventory > Stock: find a product `<CODE>` in `<WH>` with on hand of 1 or more AND open SO ABOVE its on hand (the prod case was MWC7632-SH-P in MOCHA-WH: on hand 6, O/S 13). Note the on hand as `<N>`. Read-only SQL on the dev copy to find one, with the contact's own policy warehouse:

   ```sql
   SELECT p.product_code, w.warehouse_code, s.quantity_on_hand AS on_hand,
          SUM(l.qty_ordered - l.qty_delivered) AS open_so
   FROM stock s
   JOIN warehouses w ON w.id = s.warehouse_id AND w.is_active
   JOIN products p ON p.id = s.product_id AND p.is_active
   JOIN sales_order_lines l ON l.product_id = s.product_id AND l.warehouse_id = s.warehouse_id
        AND l.line_status = 'open' AND l.qty_ordered > l.qty_delivered
   WHERE s.quantity_on_hand BETWEEN 1 AND 50
     AND s.warehouse_id = ANY (
       SELECT unnest(sp.warehouse_ids) FROM stock_visibility_policies sp
       JOIN respond_contacts c ON c.id = sp.contact_id
       WHERE c.respond_io_id = '900000039' AND sp.mode = 'availability')
   GROUP BY p.product_code, w.warehouse_code, s.quantity_on_hand
   HAVING SUM(l.qty_ordered - l.qty_delivered) > s.quantity_on_hand
   ORDER BY s.quantity_on_hand
   LIMIT 10;
   ```

   If the dev copy has no such product, seed one from the console check harness instead of by hand: `documentation/plans/chatbot/evidence/avail-onhand-basis/harness/seed_onhand.py` (writes MWC7632-SH-P, 6 on hand in MOCHA-WH, an open SO line of 13, under a new contact 900000012 "Onhand Buyer" on an availability policy over MOCHA-WH; dev copy only, never production). Then DEALER = that contact, `<CODE>` = MWC7632-SH-P, `<N>` = 6.
4. Master data > Product categories: the category of `<CODE>` has a Chatbot max quantity of at least 10 (no max set reads as 0 and the line prints 🚫 instead of the stock answer). The product's own Chatbot max quantity, if set, also at least 10.
5. Inventory > Stock, staff view of `<CODE>`: note the Total, O/S and the `<WH>` line. These must read the SAME after the test (the staff figures are not changed by this lane).
6. The console is a dry run. Nothing reaches WhatsApp.
