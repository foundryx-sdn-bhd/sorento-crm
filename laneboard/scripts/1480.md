# Hand test #1480 (CONTACT-COPY-FIELDS)

Owner ask 4 Oct 2026: the contact copy (one contact's settings onto one or many contacts) also
copies location, stock check mode, companies and market segment. Copy replaces, it never merges:
each target ends with exactly the source's values.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Sign in as a superadmin (companies are copied only by someone who could set them directly).
2. Pick three contacts on the test copy you are happy to change: S (source), T1 and T2 (targets).
3. On S's page: Stock visibility card, set the answer shape to Compact, Locations to two warehouses (any two), turn on "Hide zero-quantity locations", Save. Edit the contact and set Companies to two companies. Set Market segment to Retail.
4. On T1's page: Stock visibility card, set Detailed and any one other location, Save. Companies: one company that S does NOT have. Market segment: Project.
5. T2: on its Stock visibility card press Remove if it has its own policy (so it inherits).

## Steps

1. From the sidebar open Users & Access > People > Internal Users.
2. Tick T1 and T2 in the list.
3. Click "Copy access from contact (2)".
4. In "Copy access from", search S and pick it. Read the summary box.
5. Click "Preview changes". Read the rows for T1 and T2.
6. Click "Apply to 2 contacts".
7. Open T1's page from the list. Read the Stock visibility card, Companies and Market segment.
8. Open T2's page. Read the same three.
9. Back on the list, tick T1 only and copy from S again (single copy). Preview.

## Expected

1. The contacts list opens.
2. Both rows are ticked.
3. The dialog "Copy access to 2 contacts" opens.
4. The summary lists Stock check mode `Compact`, Locations `Only <code1>, <code2>; hide zero-quantity`, Companies with S's two company names, Market segments `Retail`. "Not copied" reads `Linked customers, CS routing, Media limits, Memory` (no Companies, because you are a superadmin).
5. T1: Stock check mode `Detailed` struck, `Compact` green; Locations the old line struck and the new line green; Companies `+ <S's company T1 lacks>` and T1's own company struck; Market segments `+ Retail`, `Project` struck. T2: Stock check mode and Locations show `Inherited` struck and S's values green; Companies `+` both of S's companies; `+ Retail`.
6. The result reads `2 updated`.
7. T1's Stock visibility card shows its own policy: Compact, S's two locations, hide zero-quantity on. Companies are exactly S's two (T1's old company is gone). Market segment is Retail only.
8. T2 shows the same values as T1.
9. T1 reads `No change` ("1 already the same").
