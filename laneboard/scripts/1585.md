## Steps

1. Open the URL and sign in as the admin.
2. In the sidebar, open Supply Chain, then Project Demand, then Fulfilment Planning.
3. Find SO325703, tick it and open its board.
4. Write down the number on the Confirm button and the decision shown on lines 45 and 46.
5. Press the gear button at the top right of the board.
6. Press "Simulate confirm".
7. Read the results block under the header.
8. Reload the page (F5).
9. Go back to Fulfilment Planning, open SO410515's board, press the gear, press "Simulate confirm".
10. In the sidebar, open Supply Chain, then Project Demand, then Planning changes, and open the
    newest batch that lists SO410515.
11. STOCK_SHORT_STEP_OPEN
12. STOCK_SHORT_STEP_SAVE
13. Press the gear, press "Simulate confirm".
14. Narrow the browser window to phone width (about 375px) and repeat steps 5 to 7 on that board.
15. Undo the decision you saved in step 12 (the line's Undo), so the line is left as found.
16. In the sidebar, open System, then Outgoing Mails.
17. Sign out, sign in as the planner, open SO325703's board, press the gear.

## Expected

1. Step 5: the gear menu lists "Simulate confirm" under "Undo all".
2. Step 7: the block starts "Simulation, nothing was saved: 1 of 1 orders would confirm".
   SO325703 reads "SO325703 would confirm as revision N" (N = its current revision + 1, or 1).
   A "Would confirm:" list names lines 45 and 46. A "Would write" list shows the borrow transfers
   with whole numbers, for example "line 45: borrow 52 WH3 -> <location>" (no "52.0000"), and any
   inquiry rows ("line 45 ITEM: ORDER_BACK 52 at <location>"). If any line would be taken to
   Cancel SO it reads "would transfer N line(s) to Cancel SO", never "transferred". If an order
   would fail, each failing line reads "line N ITEM: <ExceptionName>: <message>" (or
   "line N: <message>" when it has no item code).
3. Step 8 (nothing is saved): after the reload the Confirm button shows the same number as in
   step 4, lines 45 and 46 show the same saved decisions, and no toast ever said "confirmed".
4. Step 9: SO410515 reads "would confirm as revision N" or its exact error with the line it hits.
5. Step 10 (nothing is saved): the batch row for SO410515 is still Pending; nothing reads Applied.
6. Step 13: the order reads "would confirm as revision N" and, below it, a stock-short notice for
   the line from step 12: "line N ITEM: ... only X free at <location> ..., Y short".
7. Step 14: every line of the results block wraps inside the screen; nothing is cut off and the
   page does not scroll sideways.
8. Step 16 (nothing is saved): no new outgoing mail after the row crew noted in Setup 5.
9. Step 17: the planner's gear menu has no "Simulate confirm".
