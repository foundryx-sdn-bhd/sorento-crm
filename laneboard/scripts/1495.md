   "Product: all products" in its header, still no ETA.)

   customer), or the MISS sentence with "Customer: all customers". Still no ETA.
4. The dealer incoming view for SRTWC286-SH: one "SRTWC286-SH...: ETA dd/mm/yyyy" line per variant
   with a shipment (or "No incoming"), then "Please refer to your salesman." NO "Order date:" line
   anywhere in this reply, and the week from step 1 does not limit the ETAs shown.
5. The availability answer as today ("SRTWC286-SH-NEW-200 x 500: ..." with a tick, a cross, or an
   ETA). No "Order date:" line in this reply either.
