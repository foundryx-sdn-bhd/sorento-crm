crew test copy

## Setup

1. Restart the RQ worker and the MCP server on the test copy (the reply wording comes from the MCP presenter, and the photo read runs on the worker).
2. Chatbot Console contact DEALER: a Respond contact on the "Availability only" stock policy. CONTACT A (Respond.io 900000039) from hand test #1430 is one. The prod conversation was a dealer's: the quantity question ("How many units of ...?") is asked only on the availability view, so a contact that reads full stock details (the "Jay" contact of hand test #1500 reads "Stock details found for the requested products.") cannot replay these turns. Jay is fine ONLY if that contact's stock policy is "Availability only".
3. Product SRTWC8383-RL-GY exists on the shared dev data and is discontinued (its product page shows the discontinued flag). Open Inventory > Stock and note its on-hand quantity, and Incoming stock and note whether it has an open shipment: step 2's expected line depends on these two facts.
4. The photo: the owner's own photo from the prod conversation of 6 Oct (the product label the bot read as "SRTWC8383-RL-GY and SORENTO"). If it is not at hand, step 1 has a typed stand-in.
5. For step 7 use SRT0354: on the shared dev data it is active, has nothing on hand and no open shipment (replayed live 6 Oct). Any other code with nothing on hand and no open shipment works.
6. Contact FULL VIEW for step 9: a Respond contact whose stock policy shows full stock details (not "Availability only"). On the "Availability only" view a set question is answered with the quantity question over the set's members (`How many units for each?` and a numbered list of codes), on this build and on main alike, so step 9 cannot be read there.
7. Open System management > Chatbot Console, pick DEALER. The console is a dry run; nothing reaches WhatsApp. Press Reset before each step marked Reset.

## Steps

1. Click the paperclip ("Attach image or audio") and send the photo from Setup 4 with no text. Stand-in when the photo is not at hand: type `SRTWC8383-RL-GY SORENTO`.
2. Type `1`
3. Click the "trace" link under the reply from step 2, look at the page that opens, then close that tab.
4. Type `check stock`
5. Type `2`
6. Reset.
7. Type `SRT0354 x 5`
8. Pick the FULL VIEW contact from Setup 6, then Reset.
9. Type `which basins have stock`

## Expected

1. The reply ends with the question `How many units of SRTWC8383-RL-GY?`. With the photo the reply is `I read SRTWC8383-RL-GY and SORENTO from that photo. How many units of SRTWC8383-RL-GY?` (the prod turn 120 reply). With the typed stand-in the brand word is not echoed; the question is the same.
2. The reply is ONE line for the product plus the discontinued marker, and nothing else. Which line depends on Setup 3:
   - stock on hand: `SRTWC8383-RL-GY x 1: ✅ Got stock.`
   - nothing on hand, an open shipment: `SRTWC8383-RL-GY x 1: ❌ No stock, ETA dd/mm/yyyy.` (the shipment's date)
   - nothing on hand, no shipment: `SRTWC8383-RL-GY x 1: ❌ No stock and no incoming. Please refer to your salesman.`
   The next line is `⚠️  *(PRODUCT DISCONTINUED)*`.
   Never the prod defect: no `*stock* for SRTWC8383-RL-GY:` header, no `Please refer to your salesman.` on its own line under a header, no `Couldn't find` sentence, no sentence about SORENTO, no `Here's what you want:` set header, no `_Data last updated_` footer.
3. A new tab opens on Chat History with a panel titled "Turn #..." listing the stages of that turn. The "Understood" stage reads as a stock check about inventory. The "Looked up" stage names one section for inventory and does NOT say `missed: inventory` (the prod turn's trace did).
4. The reply is the question about the SAME product: `How many units of SRTWC8383-RL-GY?`. Never `How many units for each?` over a numbered list of other codes (what this turn answered before the fix: the stray word SORENTO turned the question into one about the whole catalogue).
5. One line for the product plus the discontinued marker, for 2 units. With 1 on hand (the shared dev data on 6 Oct): `SRTWC8383-RL-GY x 2: ✅ Only 1 got stock.` then `⚠️  *(PRODUCT DISCONTINUED)*`. With 2 or more on hand: `SRTWC8383-RL-GY x 2: ✅ Got stock.`. Never a list of other codes.
6. The conversation clears.
7. Control for #1501 (a real empty stock result is still a real miss line): the reply is `SRT0354 x 5: ❌ No stock and no incoming. Please refer to your salesman.` One line. If the code has an open shipment on the copy, the ETA line prints instead; then Setup 5 names the wrong code for this copy, pick another.
8. The console shows the FULL VIEW contact and an empty conversation.
9. Control for #1502 (a real set question still returns a set), on the FULL VIEW contact: the reply opens `Here's what you want: ` followed by the described set and its count in brackets, for example `Here's what you want: wash basins with stock (541)` (the shared dev data on 6 Oct). When the count is above 50 the header is followed by one breakdown line per brand, each `• <brand> wash basins: <count>`, and no product rows; at 50 or below the product rows follow. A line `Other brands with stock: ...` may close it. Never a bare `*stock* for` header, never a single-product availability line, never `No stock found for`.
