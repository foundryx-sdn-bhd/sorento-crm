5. Press Back again.
6. Press Back again.
7. Press the browser Forward button twice.

10. In the breadcrumb, click A.
11. Press Back.
12. In the left folder tree, click a different top-level folder, then press Back.
13. Type something in the search box inside C, then press Back.
14. Edit the address bar: replace the `directoryId` value with `not-a-real-folder`, press Enter.
15. Click the Home crumb ("All files").
16. Repeat steps 2 to 6 with the window narrowed to phone width (about 375px).
