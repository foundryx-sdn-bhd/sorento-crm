# Hand test: Download selected folders and files as one ZIP (PR #1520)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Sign in as the usual owner test login (a staff user who can open Dashboards > Resources > Files).
- The queue worker must be running on the test copy (crew starts it). Without it the archive stays at "Preparing".
- Real case: the folder Marketing. On the test copy the database is a prod copy but the storage bucket is not, so many files have no stored object. The folders below were checked on the copy on 7 Oct: CATALOG NL (1 file, object present), Cabana Technical Drawing (empty folder), CATALOG SORENTO (2 files, 1 present and 1 missing), Form (23 files, 22 present), Price Tag (4 files, all missing: this is the true failed case on the copy; on production those files exist and would download).
- Marketing holds no file owned by Mocha or Cabana on the copy, so the "files outside your companies" line cannot be shown here; that rule is pinned by the automated test (shared file included, own-company included, other-company excluded and counted). The big 1374-file run (Sorento Product Photo) cannot be reproduced on the copy either because most of its objects are missing from the copy's bucket.
- A ZIP viewer on your machine (double-click opens it on macOS and Windows).

## Steps
1. Open Dashboards > Resources > Files from the sidebar and click into Marketing.
2. Tick the folders CATALOG NL and Cabana Technical Drawing.
3. Click Action in the blue selection strip and look at which items are enabled.
4. Click Download selected and watch the dialog until it finishes.
5. Click Download ZIP in the dialog, then open the downloaded ZIP on your machine.
6. Close the dialog. Click the download icon in the top bar (My downloads).
7. Click the download icon on the archive row in the drawer.
8. Back in Files, tick CATALOG SORENTO, click Action, Download selected, wait for it to finish.
9. Clear the selection, tick Price Tag, click Action, Download selected, wait.
10. Open My downloads from the top bar and look at the newest row.
11. Clear the selection, tick Form, click Action, Download selected, and click Cancel download while the bar is moving (it runs about ten seconds; if it finishes first, that is fine, the cancel is covered by the automated tests and step 12 still works).
12. Tick Form again, click Action, Download selected, and while it is preparing open a second browser tab on the same Files page, tick Form, Action, Download selected again.
13. Tick any folder, click Action, and look at Export selected.
14. Open the folder CATALOG SORENTO, tick its two files, click Action, Export selected.
15. In the sidebar open Resources > Trash, tick a deleted item and open Action.
16. Make the browser window narrow (phone width) and repeat steps 2 to 4.
17. Leave a ready archive in My downloads and come back the next day (or ask crew to run the daily purge).

## Expected
- Step 3: Download selected is enabled. Export selected is greyed out with "Not available when a folder is selected." Move to and Delete selected are enabled.
- Step 4: a dialog titled Download selected opens at once with the archive name (Marketing-<date>-<time>.zip, Malaysia time), the line "Preparing archive in the background", a Files x / y counter and a progress bar. When finished: "Archive ready", the line "1 files in 2 folders, 4 MB", the line "Also listed in My downloads (top bar). Link valid for 1 hour; the archive is removed after 24 hours.", buttons Close and Download ZIP. No spinner remains.
- Step 5: the ZIP downloads with the archive name. Inside: the folder CATALOG NL with its PDF, and an EMPTY folder Cabana Technical Drawing. No extra file such as a manifest.
- Step 6: the drawer lists the archive, label "Files archive (ZIP)", status Ready, a download icon and NO preview icon.
- Step 7: the same ZIP downloads again.
- Step 8: "Archive ready" with "1 files in 1 folders, ..." and the extra line "1 files could not be read and were skipped" (the missing object on the copy). The ZIP holds the one readable catalogue.
- Step 9: the dialog switches to "Archive failed" with the reason "No file could be read" and only a Close button. No Download ZIP, no spinner.
- Step 10: the newest row shows Failed with "No file could be read"; older rows stay Ready.
- Step 11: the dialog switches to "Download cancelled" with "Nothing was saved. Select the folders again to start a new download." and only Close. The drawer row for this run shows Failed with "Download cancelled".
- Step 12: the second request is refused with the message "A files archive is already being prepared. Wait for it to finish or cancel it." The first run keeps going and becomes Ready.
- Step 13: Export selected is greyed out with the same reason as step 3.
- Step 14: Export selected opens the usual Excel export dialog exactly as before.
- Step 15: the Trash menu offers only Restore selected and Delete selected. No Download selected.
- Step 16: at phone width the Action menu, the dialog (full width, stacked buttons) and the ready state read the same; nothing is cut off and the page does not scroll sideways.
- Step 17: after 24 hours the archive row is gone from My downloads and its link stops working. A link copied earlier stops working after 1 hour even before that.
