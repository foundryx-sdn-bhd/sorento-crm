# Hand test: Download selected folders and files as one ZIP (PR #1520)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Sign in as the usual owner test login (a staff user who can open Dashboards > Resources > Files and who belongs to ONE company, Sorento).
- The queue worker must be running on the test copy (crew starts it). Without it the archive stays at "Preparing".
- Real case: the folder Marketing. The three folders used below exist on the test copy: Sorento Product Photo (123 subfolders, 1374 files, about 1.5 GB), Price Tag (4 subfolders, 4 files, 37 MB), Cabana Technical Drawing (empty).
- For the company check: Marketing is a shared folder (Company shows Shared). Some files inside it belong to Mocha or Cabana rather than Sorento; the test copy already has such files, no seeding needed.
- A ZIP viewer on your machine (double-click opens it on macOS and Windows).

## Steps
1. Open Dashboards > Resources > Files from the sidebar and click into Marketing.
2. Tick the folders Price Tag and Cabana Technical Drawing (small, fast run).
3. Click Action in the blue selection strip and look at which items are enabled.
4. Click Download selected and watch the dialog until it finishes.
5. Click Download ZIP in the dialog, then open the downloaded ZIP on your machine.
6. Close the dialog. Click the download icon in the top bar (My downloads).
7. Click the download icon on the archive row in the drawer.
8. Back in Files, tick the folder Sorento Product Photo (the big one) and click Action, Download selected.
9. While it is preparing, click Close. Open My downloads from the top bar and watch the row.
10. When the row shows Ready, click its download icon. Open the ZIP and open the folder Sorento Product Photo inside it.
11. Tick Sorento Product Photo again, Action, Download selected, and this time click Cancel download while it is preparing.
12. Immediately tick Price Tag, Action, Download selected, and while that one is preparing, open a second browser tab on the same Files page, tick Price Tag, Action, Download selected again.
13. Tick any folder, click Action, and look at Export selected.
14. Tick only files (open the folder CATALOG SORENTO and tick its two files), click Action, Export selected.
15. Open the Trash view in the folder tree, tick a deleted item and open Action.
16. Open the ZIP from step 10 again and look for a folder whose name contains a dash where the screen shows a slash (for example a folder shown as "SRT LITE / DIY PROMOTION (END USER)" at the Marketing level is written as "SRT LITE - DIY PROMOTION (END USER)" if you include it in a run).
17. Leave the ready archive in My downloads and come back the next day (or ask crew to run the daily purge).

## Expected
- Step 3: Download selected is enabled. Export selected is greyed out with "Not available when a folder is selected." Move to and Delete selected are enabled.
- Step 4: a dialog titled Download selected opens at once with the archive name (Marketing-<date>-<time>.zip, the time is Malaysia time), the line "Preparing archive in the background", a Files x / y counter (y = 4) and a progress bar. When finished: "Archive ready", a line "4 files in 5 folders, 37 MB" (Cabana Technical Drawing counts as a folder), the line "Also listed in My downloads (top bar). Link valid for 1 hour; the archive is removed after 24 hours.", buttons Close and Download ZIP. No spinner remains.
- Step 5: the ZIP downloads with the archive name. Inside: the folder Price Tag with its 4 subfolders and 4 files, and an EMPTY folder Cabana Technical Drawing. No extra file such as a manifest.
- Step 6: the drawer lists the archive, label "Files archive (ZIP)", status Ready, a download icon and NO preview icon.
- Step 7: the same ZIP downloads again.
- Step 8: the dialog shows Files x / 1,374 (or slightly fewer, see the company line) and the bar moves for a while (about one to three minutes).
- Step 9: closing the dialog does not stop anything; the drawer row shows Preparing, then Ready on its own without reloading the page.
- Step 10: the ZIP opens and holds Sorento Product Photo with its subfolders and photos. If the dialog earlier showed "N files not included (outside your companies)", exactly those N files (Mocha or Cabana owned) are missing, and files marked Shared are present. The "Archive ready" summary for this run shows that line only when N is above zero.
- Step 11: the dialog switches to "Download cancelled" with "Nothing was saved. Select the folders again to start a new download." and only Close. The drawer row for this run shows Failed with "Download cancelled".
- Step 12: the second request is refused with the message "A files archive is already being prepared. Wait for it to finish or cancel it." The first run keeps going and becomes Ready.
- Step 13: Export selected is greyed out with the same reason as step 3.
- Step 14: Export selected opens the usual Excel export dialog exactly as before.
- Step 15: the Trash menu offers only Restore selected and Delete selected. No Download selected.
- Step 16: slashes in folder or file names are written as dashes inside the ZIP; no folder or file is missing because of it.
- Step 17: after 24 hours the archive row is gone from My downloads and its link stops working. A link copied earlier stops working after 1 hour even before that.
