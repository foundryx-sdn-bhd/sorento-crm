## URL

crew test copy

## Setup

Crew setup on the test copy of head 82d945d0 (or later). The owner runs no
commands.

1. No migration and no data change. The copy uses the shared dev DB as it is: HQ is NOT a
   project reserve there (its `segment` is not `project`), so no reserve supply shows. The
   reserve half (HQ's unlinked 5,000 PO carried to Dec-Mar) is covered by
   `test_prod_srtwt2215_with_hq_a_project_reserve_carries_the_5000` and is hand-tested with
   PR #1597, which ships `HQ.segment = project`.
2. Read-only page: nothing in these steps writes or sends anything.

## Steps

1. Open the URL and sign in as a planner who can see Stock Debt.
2. In the sidebar, open Procurement, then Supply Chain, then Stock Debt.
3. Type SRTWT2215 in the search box.
4. Open Filters and set Book to Project. Leave "Projected balance" off.
5. Write down every month cell of the SRTWT2215 row.
6. Open Filters and turn "Projected balance" on.
7. Compare every month cell of the SRTWT2215 row with step 5.
8. Open Filters and set Book to All, keeping "Projected balance" on.
9. Compare every month cell of the SRTWT2215 row with step 5 again.
10. Click the SRTWT2215 Oct cell.
11. Read the grey projection line above the tabs.
12. In the Demand tab, find SO364824's BRW-BB row and read its Covered by.
13. Open the Supply tab and find SPO-2026/09-0036 line 126 at BRW.
14. Close the drill and type SRTWT7443 in the search box (Book All, "Projected balance" on).
15. Write down every month cell, then set Book to Project and turn "Projected balance" off.
16. Compare every month cell with step 15's.
17. Turn "Projected balance" on and then off again.

## Expected

1. Step 6: a "Projected balance" chip appears. Every month cell reads exactly what it read in step 5. Nothing grows by BRW's on hand, HQ's 5,000 PO or the BRW SPO 333: none of them counts beyond what a link places on a project line.
2. Step 9: Book All with the switch on reads the same cells as step 5, although Book All with the switch off counts BRW's own on hand and orders in its months.
3. Steps 11 to 13: the projection line reads "Project bins +132 · Projected +132" and names no pool, no dealer orders and no reserve supply. SO364824's BRW-BB row is covered by SPO-2026/09-0036 line 129 for 486. In the Supply tab, SPO-2026/09-0036 line 126 (333, at BRW, unlinked) shows nothing in Assigned to.
4. Step 16: every SRTWT7443 month cell is equal in the two readings. Its unlinked open BRW POs and BRW SPO add nothing.
5. Step 17: the chip appears and then disappears; with the switch off every cell reads the plain month view again.
