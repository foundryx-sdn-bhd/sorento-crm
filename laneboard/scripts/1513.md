## Steps

1. Send: `Any WC with s trap 300mm available?`
2. Send: `I need 8152 6 unit`
3. Send: `8357 x 10`
4. Send: `SRTWC8152-SH-300 x 6 Units Have stock?`
5. Send: `10`
6. Send: `Any WC with s trap 300mm available?` then send: `stock SRTWC9999 x 5` (a code not on the list; any real code not listed works)
7. Send: `Any WC with S trap 300mm available` (capital S, no question mark: the 14:19 sentence)
8. Send: `SRTWC286-SH x 10` then send: `How about p trap`
9. Send: `any water closet 600mm wide`

## Expected

1. Step 1: the S trap 300mm water closets with stock, then `How many units for each?` with one
   numbered line per product ending ` - ` (five lines on the dev copy, nine on the shared dev
   copy, the order the tool returns).
2. Step 2: ONE line, `SRTWC8152-SH-300 x 6: <the normal availability line>`. No list, no
   "How many units", no "Not checked", no "still open" line.
3. Step 3: ONE line for `SRTWC8357-300-RL x 10`. The list is not printed again.
4. Step 4: the same one line as step 2 again (`SRTWC8152-SH-300 x 6: ...`). Never the list.
5. Step 5: EVERY line still open answered at 10 each, one line per product (three on the dev
   copy: BRC21466UW-2-MY, SRTWC8152-SH-UF-300, SRTWC8366-300-RL-UF; seven on the shared dev
   copy); never line 1 alone. SRTWC8152-SH-300 and SRTWC8357-300-RL are NOT repeated. After this the stock task is answered (a later
   "how about 20?" revises it, as before).
6. Step 6: after the list, the second message is answered as its own stock ask for that code
   (its availability line, or the usual did-you-mean when the code does not exist); the list is
   not printed again and nothing says "Not checked".
7. Step 7: exactly the same products as step 1 (the console trace shows `trap_type s_trap` and
   `trap_length 300`, never `dim_width 300`); never "no stock matched these".
8. Step 8: the P trap sibling, SRTWC286-SH-P, is the product answered (its stock line at 10, or
   `How many units of SRTWC286-SH-P?` if the follow-up carried no quantity). Never
   "P trap water closets (182)" and never a brand breakdown.
9. Step 9: a width ask still parses `dim_width 600` (control); the reply lists 600mm-wide water
   closets or says none matched. Never a trap spec.
