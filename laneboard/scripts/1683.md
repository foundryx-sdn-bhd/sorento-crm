# Hand test: PR #1683 STOCK-DEBT-SAVED-VIEWS (saved views and a Location filter on Stock Debt)

Stock Debt gets a Location filter (every location group the data holds; `Only` the picked ones
or `All except` them) that scopes the whole calculation (on hand, incoming and orders of those
locations), and the saved-views menu the Reorder plan lines already use. The owner's two views:
a "Project view" (every location except one) and a single-location view. The delivery date range
is saved as "from today", so "next 30 days" stays the next 30 days. The three BRW columns are a
fixed BRW retail-pool reference and now say so.

The dev copy has no `-ED` warehouse at all, so this test uses **IB** as ED's stand-in. In
production ED works the same way once switched on (Setup 3).

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
Crew prepares the copy. The owner runs no commands.

1. Sign in with your own admin account (SRT + MCH, active company SRT; the figures are the same
   under SRT scope and all-company scope).
2. Product used for the numbers: **B2155-NL-BLUE** (figures read by crew on the shared dev DB,
   15 Oct, through this branch's code: Book = All, no date range, Only products in debt on).
   Keep Book on `All` until step 14.
3. Production step after deploy (owner, not part of this test): to bring ED in, sidebar
   **Inventory > Warehouses**, open each active warehouse whose code ends in `-ED` (the text after
   the first hyphen is `ED`, e.g. `PRJ-ED`, `BRW-ED`), click **Edit**, switch **Fulfilment
   planning** ON, save. The list's Fulfilment planning column then reads `On`. ED appears in the
   Location list after that, with no code change; borrowing ED stock stays the user's choice on
   the fulfilment board.

## Steps
1. CRM sidebar: Procurement > Supply Chain > Stock Debt. Search `B2155-NL-BLUE`.
2. Note its Oct and Nov balances.
3. Click `Filters`. Open the `Location` select (under `Supplier`).
4. Pick `IB`. Leave the mode on `Only`.
5. Close the Filters panel and read B2155-NL-BLUE's Oct and Nov balances.
6. Click `Save view`, name it `IB only`, click `Save`.
7. Open `Filters`, switch the Location mode to `All except` (IB stays picked).
8. Close the panel and read B2155-NL-BLUE's Oct and Nov balances.
9. Click `Save view`, name it `Project view`, click `Save`.
10. Open the views menu (it reads the current view's name or `No segment`), choose `No segment`.
11. Open the views menu, pick `IB only`.
12. Open the views menu, pick `Project view`.
13. Click B2155-NL-BLUE's Nov cell.
14. Close the cell. Open `Filters`, set `Sales order delivery date` to today to today + 30 days
    (type e.g. `15/10/2026 - 14/11/2026`). Click `Save view`, name it `Project next 30 days`.
15. Open the views menu, choose `No segment`, then pick `Project next 30 days`.
16. Look at the column headers between Product and the first month, then pick `IB only` and look
    again.
17. Open the views menu, pick `Project next 30 days`, open the menu again, click `Delete view`;
    click `Cancel` in the toast. Then click `Delete view` again and let it run out.
18. Optional, on a later day: re-create `Project next 30 days` if deleted, pick it again.
