# Hand test: PR #1683 STOCK-DEBT-SAVED-VIEWS (saved views and a Location filter on Stock Debt)

Stock Debt gets back a Location filter (every location group the data holds, several at once)
that scopes the whole calculation (on hand, incoming and demand of those locations only), and
the saved-views menu the Reorder plan lines already use: save the current filters under a name,
pick a view to apply them all at once. The delivery date range is saved as "from today", so a
view saved as "next 30 days" stays the next 30 days.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
Crew prepares the copy. The owner runs no commands.

1. Sign in to the CRM as the owner.
2. ED appears in the Location list only once its bins have "Fulfilment planning" on
   (Inventory Management > Warehouses > edit the bin > `Fulfilment planning`). That switch
   puts the bin into fulfilment planning everywhere, so on the shared dev copy leave it as it
   is unless crew has already switched it on; the steps below use the locations listed.

## Steps
1. CRM sidebar: Procurement > Supply Chain > Stock Debt.
2. Click `Filters`. Look at the `Location` select under `Supplier` and open it.
3. Pick one location (e.g. `IB`).
4. Set `Book` to `Project`.
5. In `Sales order delivery date` type today to today + 30 days (e.g. `14/10/2026 - 13/11/2026`).
6. Close the Filters panel. Click `Save view` (left of the toolbar, beside the views menu that
   reads `No segment`), name it `IB next 30 days`, click `Save`.
7. Open the views menu, choose `No segment`.
8. Open `Filters`, set `Book` to `Project`, pick two locations (e.g. `BB` and `IB`). Click
   `Save view`, name it `Project view`, click `Save`.
9. Open the views menu, pick `IB next 30 days`.
10. Click any red month cell.
11. Close the cell. Open the views menu, pick `Project view`.
12. Open the views menu, pick `IB next 30 days`, open the menu again, click `Delete view`;
    click `Cancel` in the toast.
13. Click `Delete view` again and let the countdown run out.
14. Optional, on a later day: pick `IB next 30 days` again (re-create it first if deleted).

## Expected
- Step 2: the Location list shows the location groups present in the data as codes (e.g. `AM`,
  `BB`, `IB`, `IR`, `NTC`); no fixed list, and `ED` only if its bins are on.
- Step 3: chip `Location: IB`; the balances change (only IB's stock, incoming and orders count),
  they are not just fewer rows.
- Step 5: chip `Delivery: 14 Oct 26 to 13 Nov 26` (your dates).
- Step 6: the views menu reads `IB next 30 days` and lists it under `Mine`.
- Step 7: every chip is gone; the board is back to the whole book with `Only products in debt` on.
- Step 8: chip `Locations: BB, IB`.
- Step 9: one pick brings back `Book: Project`, `Location: IB` and the Delivery chip for today
  to today + 30 days.
- Step 10: the cell's demand and supply are IB's only and foot with the cell.
- Step 11: chips `Book: Project`, `Locations: BB, IB`, no Delivery chip.
- Step 12: a `Deleting` countdown with `Cancel` appears, no confirm dialog; after Cancel the view
  is still there.
- Step 13: after about 10 seconds the view is gone.
- Step 14: the Delivery chip reads the NEW today to today + 30 days, not the dates it was saved on.
