# Hand test #1499 (MENU-PERM-GATE)

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. An admin or superadmin account to sign in with (the owner's own).
2. A user whose only permission is `ideation.board.view`, ACTIVE, not an admin. On the crew
   copy: create a role "Retail Salesperson" (Users & Access > Access > Roles) holding only
   "View Ideation Board", then a user (Users & Access > People > Administrative Users > Add
   user) with that role alone. The retail-salesperson user from the 6 Oct prod report works the
   same way if that role has no other permission.
3. Not impersonating anyone yet (no yellow banner at the top).

## Steps

1. Sign in as the admin and open `/`.
2. Read the sidebar: expand System, then Platform and Operations.
3. Open Users & Access > People > Administrative Users.
4. On the retail-salesperson user's row open the actions button, choose "Impersonate user",
   confirm with "Impersonate". The page reloads with the yellow banner.
5. Read the sidebar again.
6. Open `/system-management/companies` by typing the URL.
7. Click "Exit Impersonation" in the yellow banner.
8. Read the sidebar once more.
9. Sign out, sign in as the retail-salesperson user, read the sidebar.
