# Hand test: PR #1540 LOADER-CENTER (centre the full-page Sorento loader)

## URL

crew test copy

## Setup

1. Sign out of the test copy if signed in.
2. In Chrome DevTools, Network tab, set throttling to "Slow 4G" so the loader stays up long
   enough to look at (turn it back off afterwards).

## Steps

1. Sign in on the test copy with a staff account.
2. While the white loading screen shows, look at the "Sorento" word and the grey spinner under it.
3. Reload any page inside the app (for example the dashboard) and watch the same loading screen.
4. Open DevTools device mode at 375px wide (iPhone SE size) and reload again.

## Expected

1. The spinner sits directly under the middle of the "Sorento" word (no longer pushed to the
   right of it, as in the 9 Oct screenshot).
2. The word plus spinner, taken together, sit in the middle of the page both left-right and
   top-bottom, at desktop width and at 375px.
3. The word is the same navy, weight and size as before; the spinner is the same small grey
   spinner, about 16px below the word.
4. After loading finishes the app opens exactly as before (nothing else changed).
