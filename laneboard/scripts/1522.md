# PR 1522: Decide dropdown now refreshes an open line panel

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup
- Any planner login that can open Supply Chain > Project Demand > Fulfilment Planning and save decisions.
- A sales order on the board with a line whose suggestion spans two locations. On the crew test copy (dev DB sorento_cagent_stack) SO424856 does not exist, so use SO253362 line 1 (BRWTPB865104CP, outstanding 39, suggested 9 BRW + 30 BRW-IB). On production the owner's own case is SO424856 line 16 (SRT384-6) or line 32 (TPE-9101).
- Nothing else to seed. Undo the saved decision at the end so the line is left as found.

## Steps
1. Open Supply Chain > Project Demand > Fulfilment Planning from the sidebar.
2. Search SO253362, tick its checkbox, click Plan SO253362.
3. Click List at the top of the board.
4. Click the row's Change decision icon (pencil) to open its panel. Note the Reserve boxes (9 at BRW, 30 at BRW-IB).
5. Tick the row's checkbox at the left.
6. Click Decide and choose Use own location. (Use BRW cannot cover this line on the test copy: the BRW pool holds 18, so Save stays at 0 lines.)
7. Type a reason and click Save 1 line.
8. Look at the open panel again without closing it.
9. Click the row's Undo icon in the Verdict column, still with the panel open.
10. Click Save decision in the panel.
11. Type 1 into the Reserve at BRW box.
12. Tick the row and use Decide > Use own location again with a reason, Save 1 line.
13. Click Collapse all, choose Discard, then click the row's Change decision icon again.
14. Click Undo on the row to leave the line as found.

## Expected
- Step 4: the panel shows 9 at BRW and 30 at BRW-IB; the row's Decided column reads Not decided.
- Step 8: the panel's Reserve boxes now read 0 at BRW and 39 at BRW-IB, the Reason box holds the reason you typed, the button reads Saved, and the Decided column reads Own 39 (BRW-IB).
- Step 9: the panel goes back to 9 / 30, the Reason box is empty, the button reads Save decision, the Decided column reads Not decided.
- Step 10: the save lands with no error; the toast says 1 to confirm; the button reads Saved. No message about components adding up to the wrong number.
- Step 12: the box you typed into still reads 1 (the panel is NOT overwritten), while the Decided column already reads Own 39 (BRW-IB).
- Step 13: the reopened panel reads 0 / 39 with the step 12 reason.
- Everything above also reads cleanly at phone width (375px): the Reserve boxes are fully visible and the page does not scroll sideways.
