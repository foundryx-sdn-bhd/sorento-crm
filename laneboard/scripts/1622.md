# Hand test: PR #1622 OI-REJECT-NOTIFY (email + in-app to the OI raiser when rows are rejected, one per OI)

## URL

crew test copy

## Setup

Crew setup on the test copy of this PR's head (the sha in the latest crew-done comment). The owner
runs no commands.

1. Apply the THIRD (latest) crew-migration SQL from the PR; it replaces both earlier ones:
   template `order_inquiry_rejected_default` with the single `Open sales order` button,
   automation `Order inquiry rejected`, and the email theme's company set to Sorento where System
   Settings still says Metronic. Check: System, Automations lists "Order inquiry rejected",
   enabled.
2. SMTP: the dev DB's `system_settings` carries `smtp_host=asdfmail.sorento.com.myxx:465`, which
   does not resolve, and the copy's worker drains the `notifications` queue. So every mail this
   test creates is attempted and ends with status `failed` in Email Outbox (never `queued`,
   never delivered). The row, its recipient and its rendered preview are what this test reads;
   the `failed` status is expected and is not a defect of this PR.
3. Crew notes the newest row in System, Messaging, Outgoing Mails, Email Outbox before step 1,
   so new rows are easy to tell apart.
4. Pick two open Order Inquiries raised by an ACTIVE user who is NOT the owner (for example
   Eling): OI-A with at least 3 rows in state Raised, Partly linked or Placed that are not yet
   rejected, OI-B with at least 1 such row. Note both OI numbers, their SO numbers and customers.
   Also pick one OI the owner raised themselves (OI-C) with one such row; skip steps 10 and 11
   if none exists.
5. The owner signs in as themselves (holds Order Inquiries acknowledge). Crew provides a way to
   sign in as the raiser for steps 8 to 9e.

## Steps

1. Open the URL, sign in, open Procurement, then Supply Chain, then Order Inquiries.
2. Open OI-A and tick 2 of its rows on the Lines tab.
3. Open Order inquiry options, choose Reject selected, enter the reason
   `Supplier discontinued this finish`, press Reject 2 rows.
4. In the sidebar open System, then Messaging, then Outgoing Mails, then Email Outbox, and search
   `OI rejected`.
5. Press View on the newest row and read its preview.
5a. In the preview, press `Open sales order`.
6. Back in Order Inquiries, open OI-A, tick 1 more row, Reject selected with the reason
   `No stock at supplier`; then open OI-B, tick 1 row, Reject selected with the same reason.
7. Refresh Email Outbox and search `OI rejected` again.
8. Sign in as the raiser and open the notification bell.
9. Click the notice `<OI-A no>: 2 lines rejected by purchasing`.
9a. On the Sales Order that opens, scroll the Lines tab right to the `Order inquiry` column and
    press the red `Rejected` badge (or its info icon) on the first refused line.
9b. Press `Plan` on the Sales Order header. On the board, read the header, the decision strip
    and OI-A's refused lines in the List view.
9c. Still as the raiser, open the first refused line's panel, keep `Buy the whole <qty>`, type
    `Customer accepted the chrome finish` in REASON, press Save decision, then press Confirm.
9d. Press the `Decision trail` icon on that line.
9e. Open the Sales Order again (its Lines tab).
9f. Sign back in as the owner, open Order Inquiries, open the newest inquiry for OI-A's SO and
    hover the info icon in that line's Instruction cell (or open the line's History).
10. Sign back in as the owner; open OI-C, tick its row, Reject selected with the reason
    `test self`.
11. Refresh Email Outbox and search `OI rejected`.
