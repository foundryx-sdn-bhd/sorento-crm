# Hand test #1493 (AVAIL-RENDER)

Owner ask 5 Oct 2026: on an availability contact, a ✅ line says "Got stock" and no longer ends
with "Please refer to your salesman."; 🚫 and ❌ lines keep the refer. A discontinued product's
availability line and dealer ETA line carry the marker compact/detailed print.

## URL
crew test copy

## Setup

1. Pick a contact on AVAILABILITY stock access (Stock visibility card: mode Availability) whose
   locations hold stock, and open the chat console as that contact.
2. Pick four products the contact can see: A in stock with more on hand than you will ask and a
   category max above the ask; B in stock with fewer on hand than you will ask (within the max);
   C with no stock, no shipment; D with no stock and an inbound shipment that has a packing list
   and an ETA.
3. On the Products page mark A and D "Discontinued" (is_discontinued on); leave B and C live.
4. Note the on-hand of B (call it N).

## Steps

1. In the console send `check stock <A> x 5`.
2. Send `check stock <B> x <N + 10>`.
3. Send `check stock <C> x 5`.
4. Send `check stock <D> x 5`.
5. Send `ETA <D> and <C>`.
6. Open Customer asks (the stock ask rows) for this contact.
7. Send `<A> x 5 ada stok tak` and `<B> x <N + 10> ada stok tak` (Malay reply).
8. Send `<A> x 5 有库存吗` (Chinese reply).

## Expected

1. Two lines: `<A> x 5: ✅ Got stock.` then `⚠️  (PRODUCT DISCONTINUED)` directly under it. No "Please refer to your salesman." anywhere in the reply.
2. One line: `<B> x <N + 10>: ✅ Got stock, <N> available.` No refer, no marker.
3. `<C> x 5: ❌ No stock and no incoming. Please refer to your salesman.` No marker.
4. `<D> x 5: ❌ ETA dd/mm/yyyy.` then `⚠️  (PRODUCT DISCONTINUED)` under it. No refer.
5. `<D>: ✅ ETA dd/mm/yyyy` then `⚠️  (PRODUCT DISCONTINUED)`, a blank line, `<C>: No ETA`, a blank line, `Please refer to your salesman.` (once).
6. Rows exist for step 3 (no_incoming) only among steps 1 to 4; no row for step 1 or step 2 (in stock, not referred) and none for step 4 (incoming). The salesman notification (if the contact's toggle is on) went out for step 3 only.
7. `<A> x 5: ✅ Stok ada.` with `⚠️  (PRODUK DIHENTIKAN)` under it; `<B> x <N + 10>: ✅ Stok ada, <N> tersedia.`
8. `<A> x 5: ✅ 有库存。` with `⚠️  (产品已停产)` under it.
