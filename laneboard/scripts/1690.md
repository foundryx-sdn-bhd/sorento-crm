## Setup

1. Sign in with your own admin account (one that can open Products and edit a product).
2. Crew has already applied this branch's migration SQL (the `kind` column) to the dev DB and run the loader on it:
   `venv/bin/python scripts/oneoff/link_mirror_shelf_flyer.py --apply` from `sorento_crm_backend/`. There is nothing for you to run.
3. Products used (real dev codes): cabinet SRTBF11404, mirror SRTMR11404-BL, shelf SRTSV11404-BL, cabinet SRTBF11417 (its mirror SRTMR11417 and shelf SRTSV11417 are not on dev), WC SRTWC8383-RL-MBL (seat cover link from the seat cover flyer).

## Steps

1. From the sidebar open Products > All Products.
2. Search `SRTBF11404` and open it.
3. Open the Variants tab and scroll to the Compatible section.
4. Go back to All Products, search `SRTMR11404-BL` and open it, Variants tab, Compatible section.
5. Go back, search `SRTBF11417` and open it, Variants tab, Compatible section.
6. Go back, search `SRTWC8383-RL-MBL` and open it, Variants tab, Compatible section.
7. Go back, search `SRTBF11404` and open it, Variants tab. Click Add compatible.
8. Under Link type pick `Shelf / cabinet`. Under The other product is a, pick `Shelf`. Pick `SRTSV11406-WH` and click Add.
9. On the new SRTSV11406-WH row click Unlink and let the countdown finish.

## Expected

1. The product list opens.
2. The SRTBF11404 page opens.
3. The section reads `Compatible (2)`. `Mirrors that fit` lists SRTMR11404-BL and `Shelves that fit` lists SRTSV11404-BL, both with a `Flyer` badge. There is no `No seat covers` text.
4. `Fits these cabinets` lists 7 cabinets, all `Flyer`: SRTBF11404, SRTBF11405, SRTBF11412, SRTBF11413-MBL, SRTBF11501, SRTBF11705, SRTBF11707.
5. `Compatible` with the empty states `No seat covers` and `No WCs`: nothing was linked, because the flyer's SRTMR11417 and SRTSV11417 are not on dev.
6. Same as before this change: `Seat covers that fit` lists its seat cover, `Fits these WCs` shows `No WCs`.
7. The Add compatible dialog opens with a `Link type` choice (`Seat cover / WC`, `Mirror / cabinet`, `Shelf / cabinet`) already on `Mirror / cabinet`.
8. A `Link added` toast. `Shelves that fit` now lists SRTSV11404-BL (`Flyer`) and SRTSV11406-WH (`Manual`). The section reads `Compatible (3)`.
9. An `Unlinking` countdown with Cancel, then `Link removed`. The section is back to `Compatible (2)`.
